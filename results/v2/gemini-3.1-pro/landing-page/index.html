We do not cancel noise.
We cultivate stillness.

A room is an instrument. Current acoustic solutions suffocate sound, rendering spaces flat and dead. Jaku introduces a material philosophy where excess energy is captured, but natural architectural resonance remains.

Silence is not empty.
It is the presence of space.

Below the threshold of hearing lies profound warmth. Our geometric structures dissipate sonic chaos into thermal whispers, leaving only the purest articulation of the environment and the self.

MATERIAL
SYNTHESIS

A collision of heritage & physics

01 // The Physics

Our metamaterial cores are synthetically mapped to destructively interfere with human-range frequency bands. Pushing sound into a sub-millimeter labyrinth, eliminating echo without asphyxiating the room.

02 // The Craft

Industrial sound-baffling is inert and visually parasitic. We conceal our arrays behind pristine Kurotani Washi—a 100-year-old paper-making tradition. Its organic irregularities act as an analog diffuser.
